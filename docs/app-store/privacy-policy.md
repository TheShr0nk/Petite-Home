# Privacy policy

**Petite Home Co.** · petitehome.co · Effective October 15, 2026

This policy covers the Petite Home iOS app and the petitehome.co website. It's written to be read, not skimmed past. The short version: your family's information lives in your own iCloud account, we can't see it, and the only things we collect are an email address if you give it to us, a record of your subscription, and anonymous usage counts.

## What stays on your phone and in your iCloud

Everything you enter in the app — contacts, children's details, medical notes, insurance, documents, calendars, tasks, meals, plans and the vault — is stored on your device and in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. It syncs between your devices and, if you invite them, your partner's, through Apple's own sharing. Petite Home Co. has no server that receives this data and no way to read it.

The vault is encrypted on your phone with a key that's stored in your household's iCloud record with Apple's end-to-end encryption, and cached in your iCloud Keychain. Gate and alarm codes use a separate key that never leaves your device. We can't recover vault contents if you lose access to your iCloud account.

There is no Petite Home account and no sign-in. The app identifies you by your iCloud user record, which we never receive.

## What we do collect

**Email address.** If you type your email on the Family File reveal screen or on petitehome.co, we add it to our email list along with the age range of your youngest child, so we can send you the Family File by email and occasional notes from Petite Home Co. The list is hosted by Klaviyo. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete the address at any time.

**Subscription records.** Premium purchases run through Apple's App Store and RevenueCat, which keeps a record of your subscription status tied to an anonymous ID (and your iCloud record name, so your purchase follows you across devices). We don't receive your payment details; Apple handles those.

**Anonymous usage.** We use PostHog to count things like which screens are opened and whether onboarding was finished, so we can see what's working. These events are not linked to your name or email and are not used for advertising. We don't track you across other apps or websites.

**Print orders.** If you order a printed binder through the website, we receive the shipping details you enter and the PDF you choose to print, so the order can be fulfilled. Order data is kept only as long as needed to ship and support the order.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell or rent your information.
- We don't show ads, and we don't share data with ad networks.
- We don't track you across apps, and the app doesn't ask for App Tracking Transparency permission because there's nothing to track.
- We don't collect anything from your contacts, calendar, reminders, camera or photos beyond what you choose to put in the app, and that data stays in your iCloud.

## Permissions the app asks for

Each is optional and asked for only when you use the feature:

- **Contacts** — to add emergency contacts without retyping them
- **Camera** — to read your insurance card and, with Premium, add documents to the vault
- **Photos** — to add a photo of a document to the vault
- **Calendars** — so your partner can see when you're busy and plans can go on your calendar
- **Reminders** — to put house tasks in a Petite Home list in the Reminders app
- **Face ID** — to open the vault
- **Notifications** — for expiration reminders and task nudges

You can change any of these in the iPhone Settings app.

## Sharing you control

- **Partner sharing** uses iCloud sharing. Your partner sees the household, including the vault, once they accept.
- **Trusted-person links** share a PDF of the Family File through iCloud. You choose the expiry, or make the link standing; a standing link updates when you edit the file. You can revoke a link from the app at any time.
- **Sitter sheets** are text you send yourself, built from the Family File. They never include codes or account details.

## Children

Petite Home is for parents and guardians. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 directly. Information about your children that you enter in the app stays in your iCloud, as described above.

## Deleting your data

In the app, Settings → "Delete this household" removes everything from your phone and from iCloud. To remove your email from our list, use the unsubscribe link or write to privacy@petitehome.co. To delete subscription records held by RevenueCat, write to the same address and we'll process it within 30 days.

## Third parties we use

- Apple (iCloud/CloudKit, App Store, Family Sharing)
- RevenueCat — subscription management
- PostHog — anonymous product analytics
- Klaviyo — email list
- Vercel — hosts petitehome.co

Each has its own privacy policy. None of them receive the contents of your Family File, Planner or vault.

## Where data is processed

Petite Home Co. is based in Colorado, USA. Our email list and analytics are processed in the United States. If you use the app outside the US, your iCloud data is handled by Apple under your Apple account's region.

## Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app's What's new notes and on this page, with the new effective date at the top.
