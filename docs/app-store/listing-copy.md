## Description
(2,290/4000 characters)

Petite Home puts everything someone would need to care for your kids in one place, shared with your partner, ready to hand to a sitter or grandparent in one tap.

Start with the Family File. It's free, it takes about five minutes, and it answers the questions that matter when you're not there: who to call, who the doctor is, what the kids are allergic to, where the insurance card is, and who takes care of them if you can't.

THE FAMILY FILE (free)
◦ Emergency contacts pulled straight from your phone
◦ Each child's doctor, allergies, medications and routines
◦ Insurance cards read by the camera, no retyping
◦ A completeness score so you know what's still missing
◦ Print it, AirPrint it, or order a bound binder
◦ Share a standing link with a guardian that stays current as you edit

PREMIUM
Premium keeps this for you.
◦ Reminders before passports, car seats, prescriptions and insurance expire
◦ An encrypted vault for birth certificates, Social Security cards and account details, opened with Face ID
◦ The Planner: both partners' calendars, house tasks, meals and plans on one week
◦ Date nights and trips with a sitter status, and a free-evening finder that checks both calendars
◦ A sitter sheet built from the Family File, sent by text, with no codes or account details in it
◦ Tasks mirrored into the Reminders app, plans and meals into your calendar
◦ A recipe box that imports from any recipe page and plans the week's dinners

BUILT FOR TWO
Your partner joins the household through iCloud sharing. No sign-up, no password, no account to manage. Everything stays in your own iCloud; we never see it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
There's no login. Your data lives in your iCloud account and syncs between your devices and your partner's. The vault is encrypted on the phone before it's stored. We don't track you across apps.

Petite Home Co. is a home brand for families with kids, started by a pediatric ICU nurse who spent years watching parents try to remember these answers under pressure.

Premium is a subscription, billed monthly or yearly, with a 99 cent first week. One subscription covers your partner through Apple Family Sharing. Cancel anytime in Settings.
